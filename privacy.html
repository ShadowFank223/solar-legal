Privacy Policy

This explains what the Solar Fishing Wiki and the Solar Fishing Discord apps (Solar and Solar Wiki) use and why. We don't sell anything or show ads.

Reading the wiki

You can read without logging in. No account and no tracking cookies are used for reading.

Logging in (Owners and Editors)

  • "Editor log in" uses Discord. We get your Discord user ID, name and profile picture, and your roles in the Solar Fishing server (to check you may edit).
  • This is kept in a signed cookie on your device for up to 7 days, or until you log out. We don't get your email or password.

Edits and pictures

When you edit a page or add a picture, your Discord ID and name are saved with it in the page history, which everyone can see.

The Discord apps

  • When you use a command (for example /wiki, /fust or /player), the app gets what Discord sends with it: your Discord ID and name, the server, the channel and what you typed. This is used to answer you.
  • The Solar app also helps run the server (for example moderation, logs, roles and linking your Roblox account), using the server and Roblox information needed for that.

Where it is kept

The wiki and the apps run on Cloudflare. Discord and Roblox handle their own data under their own privacy policies.

Removing your information

Ask the team in the Solar Fishing Discord server and we'll remove or change your information where we can.
